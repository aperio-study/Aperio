Study mode

Adjustable focus timers, ambient sound and chimes at 10 and 5 minutes left.

Exam mode

Full-screen exam paper, a countdown you set, and the answer sheet locked until you submit.

Calendar

Plan study sessions, practice exams and real test dates, with your PDFs ready beforehand.

Progress

Grade each exam and watch your scores and daily study hours build up by subject.

First launch of the desktop app

The apps are not code-signed yet, so your computer asks for confirmation once. Mac: right-click Aperio and choose Open, then Open again. Windows: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Install from the browser

  1. Open Aperio in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  2. Chrome and Edge: press the Install app button inside Aperio. Safari on Mac: choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. It opens in its own window and keeps working offline.
